Our Technology Foundation

LESA — Lesen Asli Sijil Asli

LESA is our in-house framework for developing reliable digital assets and document authentication solutions.

Built on cryptographic hashing and integrity verification, LESA leverages Ethereum and is designed to integrate with other blockchain platforms, including Hyperledger.

LESA provides a foundation for developing solutions where digital records need to be authenticated, verified and independently checked.

From document authentication to trusted digital infrastructure, LESA helps turn digital information into verifiable records.
